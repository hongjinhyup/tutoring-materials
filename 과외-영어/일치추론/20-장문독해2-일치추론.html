1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  The manager of the Humane Society was out of town for two weeks, and Elaine was placed in charge. Since she had taken on this responsibility before, she was familiar with the challenges she might encounter — impounding stray animals, reuniting pets with their owners, informing people their pet hadn't been found, accepting animals from those who could no longer care for them, or turning animals away when the shelter was full. Though she found the work demanding, she handled these situations with confidence and calm.

  On the first day of the manager's absence, everything was running smoothly until a woman arrived with a cat and asked to leave it at the shelter. Elaine's first question was whether the cat was a stray or not, since the shelter was obligated to take in strays but could refuse owned pets. The woman said it was her cat and explained that she was moving and couldn't take it with her — a common excuse. Although the shelter sometimes accepted pets in such cases, it was already housing over ninety cats, nearly double its capacity of fifty, so Elaine had to politely refuse.

  Elaine calmly explained that there was no space available, but the woman became visibly upset and said, "If you don't take this cat, I'll just have to let it go somewhere." Although such statements were sadly not unusual, they always troubled Elaine. Still, she maintained professionalism, offered contact information for other shelters, and tried to help. The woman complained the alternatives were too far and repeated she had no other choice. Elaine then suggested the shelter might find a volunteer to transport the cat. In response, the woman, showing her frustration toward her, placed the cat's kennel in front of her, raised her voice, and left the shelter.

  Elaine could no longer hold it in. She gently put the cat in a kennel and brought it to the back isolation area. Then she went to the back room and called the absent manager, who encouraged her to let it all out. Elaine shared what happened and released her emotions while the manager listened. She felt much better afterward and was relieved to have someone she could talk to without fear of judgment. Since then, the manager has encouraged staff to come to her if they need to release emotions kept in at work — and she listens.

* impound: 수용하다, 가둬 두다   ** stray: 길 잃은   *** kennel: 이동장   **** obligate: 의무를 지우다

1 Ex 1 해설 — 내용 일치

정답: ⑤ The shelter was holding roughly twice the number of cats it was designed for.

보호소는 수용 정원의 약 두 배에 달하는 고양이를 데리고 있었다 — "over ninety cats, nearly double its capacity of fifty"의 정확한 paraphrase.

핵심 어휘 분석
어휘뜻일치추론 포인트
capacity수용력, 최대 정원"nearly double its capacity of fifty" = 정원 50의 두 배 → ⑤ 정답 근거
obligate의무를 지우다strays는 의무적으로 수용, owned pets는 refuse 가능 — ②의 거절 '이유' 오독 주의
politely refuse정중히 거절하다자리가 없어 거절 → ① "immediately agreed" 모순
hold ~ in / let ~ out참다 / 표출하다감정을 참다가 매니저에게 'let it all out' → ④ "spoke to no one" 모순
release emotions감정을 풀어놓다매니저는 감정 표출을 권유 → ③ "reject all requests" 모순

선지별 분석

  • X ① When the woman first arrived, Elaine immediately agreed to take in her cat. 정반대. 보호소가 이미 정원의 두 배를 수용 중이어서 Elaine은 "had to politely refuse"했다. "immediately agreed"는 행동 오독 함정.
  • X ② Elaine turned the cat away because she doubted the woman was really moving. 거절의 '이유' 오독. 거절 이유는 의심(doubt)이 아니라 공간 부족("no space available", 정원 초과)이다. 이사 이야기는 'a common excuse'로 언급될 뿐 거절 사유가 아니다.
  • X ③ Over the phone, the manager told Elaine to firmly reject all such requests in the future. 동기 오독. 매니저가 전화로 한 것은 '감정을 다 쏟아내라(let it all out)'는 격려이지, 향후 요청을 거절하라는 지시가 아니다. 매니저는 이후 직원들에게도 감정 표출을 권장한다.
  • X ④ Elaine kept her distress to herself and spoke to no one about it. 모순. Elaine은 부재중 매니저에게 전화해 감정을 released하고 'let it all out'했다. "spoke to no one"은 인물 행동 오독 함정.
  • O ⑤ The shelter was holding roughly twice the number of cats it was designed for. 완전 일치. "over ninety cats, nearly double its capacity of fifty"를 'roughly twice the number it was designed for'로 정확히 paraphrase. 유일하게 본문이 직접 뒷받침한다.
함정 이야기형 지문의 일치추론은 인물의 '행동'과 '동기'를 바꿔치기하는 함정이 핵심이다. ①(거절→동의), ②(공간 부족→의심), ③(감정 표출 격려→거절 지시)처럼 본문에 등장하는 단어를 그대로 쓰되 방향만 비튼다. 각 선지마다 근거 문장을 본문에서 직접 찾아 대조하라.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  One evening, little William was on his way home from his friend's. He was passing through the market. On his way, he came across the beautiful aroma of some meatballs sold by an elderly vendor just across the road. The smell was so pleasant that little William had to stop by the stand to purchase some meatballs. However, the meatballs didn't have a listed price, so he decided to head directly home. Just as little William turned to leave, a firm voice stopped him.

  It was the elderly vendor. "Wait! You can't just leave. Before you go, you have to pay," he said. Little William was surprised at the statement made by the elderly vendor. He asked him, "Payment for what, Sir? I didn't purchase any meatballs." The vendor insisted that although little William did not purchase any meatballs, he enjoyed the pleasant smell of his meatballs for quite a long time. He truly believed that little William had to pay for it because smelling was as good as eating.

  Little William was upset about this. He didn't know how merely smelling the meatballs meant that he had to pay for them. After observing the man for a short while, little William dipped his hands in his pocket and jingled a few coins. The elderly vendor was excited to hear the coin sound. He said, "Now hurry up, boy. Pay the money fast." Little William replied, "I have already paid the money, Sir. You didn't understand?"

  The shopkeeper angrily shouted, "When did you pay the money? You reached into your pocket but came out empty-handed!" Little William made it clear that he had just paid, reminding the shopkeeper that he had heard the jingle of the coins from his pocket. He added that if smelling is as good as eating, then hearing the sound of the coins should also be as good as receiving them. He smiled proudly and walked home from there, leaving the man completely surprised.

* jingle: (딸랑거리는) 소리; 딸랑거리다

  • ① William stopped at the stand because the smell of the meatballs was so pleasant.
  • ② William started for home without buying anything because the meatballs had no listed price.
  • ③ William took a few coins out of his pocket and handed them to the vendor as payment.
  • ④ The vendor demanded payment on the grounds that William had enjoyed the smell for a long time.
  • ⑤ William claimed that hearing the coins jingle was as good as the vendor receiving them.

2 Ex 2 해설 — 내용 불일치

정답: ③ William took a few coins out of his pocket and handed them to the vendor as payment.

William은 주머니 속에서 동전을 딸랑거리기만 했을 뿐, 동전을 꺼내 건네지 않았다 — "came out empty-handed". 그래서 ③는 본문과 모순(불일치)이다.

핵심 어휘 분석
어휘뜻일치추론 포인트
jingle(동전이) 딸랑거리다'jingled a few coins'는 '소리를 냈다'일 뿐 '건넸다'가 아님 → ③ 거짓의 핵심
empty-handed빈손의"came out empty-handed" — 동전을 실제로 꺼내지 않았다는 결정적 단서
listed price표시된 가격가격 미표시 → 그냥 귀가 결정 → ② 참
as good as~이나 다름없는smelling=eating → hearing=receiving 대구 → ⑤ 참
insist / for a long time주장하다 / 오랫동안오래 냄새를 즐겼다는 이유로 지불 요구 → ④ 참

선지별 분석

  • 일치 ① William stopped at the stand because the smell of the meatballs was so pleasant. 참. "The smell was so pleasant that little William had to stop by the stand." 멈춘 이유가 향기임을 정확히 반영.
  • 일치 ② William started for home without buying anything because the meatballs had no listed price. 참. "the meatballs didn't have a listed price, so he decided to head directly home." 가격 미표시 → 미구매 귀가.
  • 불일치 ③ William took a few coins out of his pocket and handed them to the vendor as payment. 거짓 = 정답. William은 동전을 jingled(소리만 냄)했을 뿐, "reached into his pocket but came out empty-handed." 동전을 건넨 적 없다. '소리=지불'이라는 그의 논리를 '실제 건넴'으로 오독한 함정.
  • 일치 ④ The vendor demanded payment on the grounds that William had enjoyed the smell for a long time. 참. 노점상은 William이 "enjoyed the pleasant smell ... for quite a long time"이므로 지불해야 한다고 주장했다.
  • 일치 ⑤ William claimed that hearing the coins jingle was as good as the vendor receiving them. 참. "if smelling is as good as eating, then hearing the sound of the coins should also be as good as receiving them." William의 반박 논리 그대로.
포인트 불일치 문제에서 거짓 선지는 본문의 핵심어를 그대로 쓰되 행동의 결과를 한 단계 비튼다. ③는 'jingled(소리만 냄)'를 'handed(건넴)'로 바꿔 결말을 뒤집은 것 — 결정적 반증은 "came out empty-handed" 한 구절이다. 동사 하나의 차이가 정답을 가른다.
