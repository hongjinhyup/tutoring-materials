1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  Sharing meanings and experiences is not a simple, automatic process: whenever two or more people observe or participate in the same ongoing event they experience it from different perspectives, with different histories, with different background knowledge. These differences are most glaring when the individuals involved include an infant or very young child and an adult, although an adult arranging an event for an infant may find it difficult to keep these differences in mind. When giving a bath, for example, a parent whose experience with water play has been fun, whose knowledge about the source of the water and its disposition provides confidence, and whose body control ensures that the baby is safely held and not in danger, expects the infant to enjoy splashing water. Yet the baby may make it clear (perhaps by terrified crying) that her perspective is quite different from that of the adult. The two share an experience, but not a meaning.

* glaring: 두드러진   ** disposition: 성질

  • ① Sharing experiences with another person is generally an effortless and instinctive process.
  • ② A parent's knowledge of the water's temperament is what makes the baby feel reassured.
  • ③ Even when an adult and a baby go through the very same event, the significance each attaches to it can diverge.
  • ④ The baby ends up crying because the bathwater is in fact genuinely dangerous to her.
  • ⑤ The gap in perspective between two adults is wider than that between an adult and an infant.

선지별 분석

  • X ① Sharing experiences ... is generally an effortless and instinctive process. 함정 단어 automatic — 뉘앙스 반전. 원문은 "is not a simple, automatic process"로, 공유가 자동적이지 않다고 못 박는다. effortless·instinctive는 automatic의 동의어로, "not"을 놓치면 고르게 되는 정반대 선지.
  • X ② A parent's knowledge of the water's temperament is what makes the baby feel reassured. 함정 단어 disposition/confidence — 주체 혼동. 물의 성질(temperament=disposition)에 대한 지식은 부모에게 confidence를 줄 뿐, 아기를 안심시킨다는 언급은 없다. 오히려 아기는 겁에 질려 운다.
  • O ③ Even when an adult and a baby go through the very same event, the significance each attaches to it can diverge. 정답. 마지막 문장 "The two share an experience, but not a meaning"의 paraphrase. same event = share an experience, significance ... diverge = not a meaning. 첫 문장 "Sharing meanings ... is not automatic"과도 정확히 호응한다.
  • X ④ The baby ends up crying because the bathwater is in fact genuinely dangerous to her. 함정 단어 danger — 직역 함정. 원문은 body control이 아기가 "safely held and not in danger"임을 보장한다고 한다. 울음은 실제 위험이 아니라 관점 차이(perspective is quite different)의 표현이다.
  • X ⑤ The gap ... between two adults is wider than that between an adult and an infant. 함정 단어 most glaring — 비교 반전. 원문은 차이가 "infant ... and an adult"를 포함할 때 가장 두드러진다고 한다. 어른-어른 비교는 등장하지 않으며, 어른-유아 차이가 가장 크다는 본문과 모순.
풀이 전략 내용 일치 문제는 정답 선지조차 축어(verbatim)가 아니라 paraphrase로 제시된다(③의 same event = share an experience). 오답은 핵심어 하나만 비틀어 만든다 — 부정어(not automatic) 누락, 주체 혼동(부모↔아기), 직역 함정(not in danger→위험), 비교 반전(most glaring). 선지마다 근거 문장 한 곳을 본문에서 직접 찾아 대조하라.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  A detailed anthropological study of daily scientific practice in the Salk Institute laboratory in La Jolla, California shows how difficult it is to assign credit and dates. Bruno Latour describes how scientists arrived at the elucidation of TRF (thyrotropin-releasing factor), a hormone that is secreted by the brain. He says that his observations led him to “the conviction that a body of practices widely regarded by outsiders as well organized, logical, and coherent, in fact consists of a disordered array of observations with which scientists struggle to produce order.” The final consensus formulation of TRF (i.e., when it became a scientific “fact”) emerged some time between January 1968 and January 1970. Half of the Nobel Prize in Medicine for 1977 for this discovery was shared between the Salk group and another competing group in New Orleans (the other half went to Rosalind Yalow for the techniques she developed that were used in these studies), but each of these two competing groups strongly felt that they deserved sole credit and that the other did not deserve even a share of the award.

* elucidation: 규명, 설명   ** secrete: 분비하다   *** array: 배열

  • ① Latour concluded that scientific practice, though seen as orderly by outsiders, is in reality a disordered set of observations.
  • ② TRF is a hormone that is released by the thyroid gland.
  • ③ The point at which TRF became an accepted scientific fact cannot be fixed to one precise date.
  • ④ Rosalind Yalow received her share of the prize for techniques she had developed and that were used in the research.
  • ⑤ Despite jointly receiving the award, each rival group believed it alone deserved the credit.

선지별 분석

  • 일치 ① Latour concluded that scientific practice ... is in reality a disordered set of observations. 검증 핵심어 in fact / disordered array. 원문 "widely regarded by outsiders as well organized ... in fact consists of a disordered array of observations"의 정확한 paraphrase. 겉모습(orderly)과 실제(disordered)를 옳게 구분해야 참임을 알 수 있다.
  • 불일치 ② TRF is a hormone that is released by the thyroid gland. 정답(유일한 거짓). 오독 핵심어 brain. 원문은 "a hormone that is secreted by the brain"으로, TRF는 뇌에서 분비된다. thyrotropin이라는 명칭이 '갑상선(thyroid)'을 연상시켜 만든 함정으로, 분비 기관을 갑상선으로 비틀어 본문과 모순된다.
  • 일치 ③ The point at which TRF became ... fact cannot be fixed to one precise date. 검증 핵심어 some time between. 원문 "emerged some time between January 1968 and January 1970" — 2년 범위로만 표현되어 정확한 단일 날짜로 특정할 수 없다는 뜻. 글 첫 문장의 "difficult to assign ... dates"와도 호응.
  • 일치 ④ Rosalind Yalow received her share ... for techniques she had developed and that were used in the research. 검증 핵심어 the other half / techniques. 원문 "the other half went to Rosalind Yalow for the techniques she developed that were used in these studies"의 정확한 일치.
  • 일치 ⑤ Despite jointly receiving the award, each rival group believed it alone deserved the credit. 검증 핵심어 sole credit. 원문 "each of these two competing groups strongly felt that they deserved sole credit and that the other did not deserve even a share"의 paraphrase. 공동 수상(shared) ↔ 단독 공로 주장(sole)의 대비를 옳게 읽어야 참임이 확인된다.
풀이 전략 불일치 문제는 참인 선지 4개도 모두 핵심어를 정확히 이해해야 참이 확인되도록 비틀려 있다(in fact·some time between·sole credit). 거짓 1개(②)는 자명한 오류가 아니라 고유명사 속 어근(thyro-)을 미끼로 분비 기관을 바꾼 함정이다. 의학·과학 지문은 명칭의 어근보다 본문이 직접 서술한 사실(secreted by the brain)을 우선하라.
