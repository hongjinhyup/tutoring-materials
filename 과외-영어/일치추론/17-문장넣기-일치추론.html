17. 문단 속 문장 넣기 — 일치추론

26005-0084 원문 p.102 · Ex 1 難 상

1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  Deep work is when you perform an activity with deep concentration that pushes your cognitive capabilities, and by doing so create new value and improve your skills. These efforts are hard to replicate; and to be able to do deep work, we need a distraction-free environment. For many people though, we spend far too much time on the other end of the scale, doing what is referred to as shallow work. This style of working is not cognitively demanding but is rather an execution of logical-type tasks that rarely create new value (although they may still be important). We often perform these tasks while we are distracted. Shallow work has its place, both because routine-type tasks need to be done and because we cannot spend our entire time doing deep work. The problem with too much shallow work is that we feel busy, maybe even stressed, but we rarely see any output reflecting our experienced effort.

* replicate: 재현하다, 반복하다   ** execution: 실행, 수행

  • ① Shallow work demands a high level of cognitive effort because of its logical nature.
  • ② The new value produced through deep work can be readily copied by other people.
  • ③ People tend to carry out deep-work tasks even while they are distracted.
  • ④ Doing shallow work is partly justified because one cannot spend all of one's time on deep work.
  • ⑤ Shallow work produces no value whatsoever, so it ought to be eliminated.

1 Ex 1 해설 — 내용 일치

정답: ④ Doing shallow work is partly justified because one cannot spend all of one's time on deep work.

얕은 작업을 하는 것은, 모든 시간을 깊은 작업에만 쓸 수는 없기 때문에 부분적으로 정당화된다.

핵심 어휘 분석 — 함정 단어의 정확한 뜻 vs 오독
단어 (각주)정확한 뜻오독하면 빠지는 함정
replicate (각주)재현하다, 반복하다 → hard to replicate = "재현하기 어렵다""쉽게 복제·모방 가능"으로 정반대 오독 → ②
(not) cognitively demanding인지적으로 부담을 주지 않는 (셸로우 워크의 특징)"logical-type" 만 보고 "demanding(부담됨)"으로 오독 → ①
distracted산만한 — these tasks(=셸로우 워크)를 할 때주어를 deep work로 바꿔 읽는 함정 → ③

선지별 분석

  • X ① Shallow work demands a high level of cognitive effort because of its logical nature. 함정 단어 demanding 오독(뉘앙스 반전). 본문은 "This style of working is not cognitively demanding"이라고 명시 — 셸로우 워크는 인지적 부담이 없다. "logical-type tasks"를 "고도의 인지 노력"으로 오해하면 걸린다.
  • X ② The new value produced through deep work can be readily copied by other people. 각주 단어 replicate 오독(정반대). 본문 "These efforts are hard to replicate" — 재현이 어렵다. "readily copied(쉽게 복제됨)"는 정반대 진술이다.
  • X ③ People tend to carry out deep-work tasks even while they are distracted. 함정 단어 distracted 주어 혼동. 산만하게 수행하는 것은 "these tasks" 즉 셸로우 워크이며, 딥 워크는 오히려 "distraction-free environment"를 요구한다. 주어를 바꿔 읽는 함정.
  • O ④ Doing shallow work is partly justified because one cannot spend all of one's time on deep work. 근거: "Shallow work has its place, ... because we cannot spend our entire time doing deep work." 셸로우 워크가 나름의 자리를 갖는 이유를 그대로 환언(paraphrase)한 정답.
  • X ⑤ Shallow work produces no value whatsoever, so it ought to be eliminated. 함정 단어 rarely / important 과장 오독. 본문은 "rarely create new value (although they may still be important)"이고 "has its place"라고 했다. "거의 없음 ≠ 전혀 없음", "없애야 함"은 본문에 없는 처방.
풀이 전략 일치 추론은 핵심어 한 단어의 정확한 뜻에서 갈린다. ① not demanding의 부정, ② hard to replicate의 방향, ③ these tasks의 지시 대상(셸로우), ⑤ rarely ≠ never를 놓치면 함정 선지를 고른다. 정답 ④는 단어를 그대로 베끼지 않고 의미만 환언한 선지임에 주목한다.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  Symbols are instrumental in helping people derive meanings from social situations. In social encounters, each person's interpretation or definition of a given situation becomes a subjective reality from that person's viewpoint. We often assume that what we consider to be "reality" is shared by others; however, this assumption is often incorrect. Subjective reality is acquired and shared through agreed-upon symbols, especially language. If a person shouts "Fire!" in a crowded movie theater, for example, that language produces the same response (attempting to escape) in all of those who hear and understand it. When people in a group do not share the same meaning for a given symbol, however, confusion results; for example, people who did not know the meaning of the word fire would not know what the commotion was about. How people interpret the messages they receive and the situations they encounter depends largely on their own perception.

* instrumental: 도구적인, 중요한 역할을 하는   ** commotion: 소동, 소란

  • ① The belief that everyone shares our own version of reality frequently turns out to be mistaken.
  • ② Subjective reality is gained and shared by means of mutually accepted symbols, language in particular.
  • ③ A shout of "Fire!" prompts the same escape response in everyone who hears it, even those who do not grasp the word's meaning.
  • ④ When members of a group attach different meanings to the same symbol, confusion arises.
  • ⑤ The way people make sense of the situations they face rests largely on their individual perception.

2 Ex 2 해설 — 내용 불일치

정답(일치하지 않는 것): ③ A shout of "Fire!" prompts the same escape response in everyone who hears it, even those who do not grasp the word's meaning.

"불(Fire)!"이라는 외침은 단어의 의미를 이해하지 못하는 사람에게도 동일한 탈출 반응을 일으킨다 → 본문과 모순.

핵심 어휘 분석 — 함정 단어의 정확한 뜻 vs 오독
단어 (각주)정확한 뜻참·거짓을 가르는 지점
hear and understand it듣고 그리고 이해하는 사람 — 두 조건을 모두 충족해야 함"이해 못 해도(regardless / even those who do not grasp)"로 읽으면 거짓 → ③(정답)
do not share the same meaning같은 의미를 공유하지 못하면 → 혼란(confusion) 발생"different meanings"로 정확히 환언 → ④(참)
agreed-upon, especially합의된 상징, 특히 언어를 통해"mutually accepted ... in particular"로 환언 → ②(참)
assumption ... often incorrect현실이 공유된다는 가정은 흔히 틀림"frequently turns out to be mistaken"으로 환언 → ①(참)

선지별 분석

  • 참 ① The belief that everyone shares our own version of reality frequently turns out to be mistaken. 근거: "We often assume that ... 'reality' is shared by others; however, this assumption is often incorrect." 가정이 흔히 틀리다는 내용을 정확히 환언. (참)
  • 참 ② Subjective reality is gained and shared by means of mutually accepted symbols, language in particular. 근거: "Subjective reality is acquired and shared through agreed-upon symbols, especially language." agreed-upon→mutually accepted, especially→in particular로 환언. (참)
  • 거짓 ③ A shout of "Fire!" prompts the same escape response in everyone who hears it, even those who do not grasp the word's meaning. 정답(불일치). 본문은 동일 반응이 나오는 대상을 "all of those who hear and understand it"로 한정 — 듣고 이해한 사람뿐이다. 게다가 바로 뒤 "people who did not know the meaning of the word fire would not know what the commotion was about"이 이를 재확인한다. "이해하지 못해도 동일 반응"은 본문과 정면 모순.
  • 참 ④ When members of a group attach different meanings to the same symbol, confusion arises. 근거: "When people in a group do not share the same meaning for a given symbol, ... confusion results." "같은 의미를 공유하지 않음 = 다른 의미를 부여함"으로 정확히 환언. (참)
  • 참 ⑤ The way people make sense of the situations they face rests largely on their individual perception. 근거: "How people interpret ... the situations they encounter depends largely on their own perception." interpret→make sense of, own→individual로 환언. (참)
풀이 전략 불일치 추론은 참 4개를 먼저 소거하고 남는 1개를 잡는다. ③의 함정은 "hear and understand"의 두 조건을 한 조건("듣기만")으로 축소한 데 있다. 같은 문단의 반례 문장(불을 모르는 사람은 소동의 이유를 모름)이 바로 그 반증이므로, 예시가 두 번 등장하면 조건의 범위를 반드시 대조한다.
