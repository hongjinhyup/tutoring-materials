1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  In the Physics, Aristotle asks whether nature acts for the sake of something. He offers an account of rain as water that rises when heated by the Sun, then cools, condenses, and falls to Earth, but is not particularly concerned with explaining rain as such. Rather, he addresses a more general question: how to account for natural objects, events, and processes that appear with regularity. He queries whether the sharpness of front teeth and the bluntness of molars is coincidental and rejects that possibility, because this arrangement occurs with regularity and that regularity is linked to purposefulness. The sharpness of front teeth allows them to tear while the bluntness of those in the back enables them to crush food; both are useful for eating. In Aristotle's view, all natural things happen in a given way and occur for the sake of something; that is the final cause.

* query: 의문을 제기하다   ** bluntness: 뭉툭함   *** molar: 어금니

  • ① Aristotle's primary purpose in the Physics was to provide a thorough explanation of how rain is formed.
  • ② Aristotle concluded that the sharpness and bluntness of teeth are ultimately a matter of mere coincidence.
  • ③ Aristotle treated the regular arrangement of teeth as a sign of purpose rather than chance.
  • ④ The blunt molars at the front of the mouth function to tear food apart before swallowing.
  • ⑤ Aristotle held that natural processes unfold by accident and serve no particular end.

1 Ex 1 해설 — 내용 일치

정답: ③ Aristotle treated the regular arrangement of teeth as a sign of purpose rather than chance.

이빨의 규칙적 배열이 우연이 아니라 목적성과 연결됨을 — "rejects that possibility ... regularity is linked to purposefulness"가 그대로 뒷받침한다.

선지별 분석

  • X ① Aristotle's primary purpose was to provide a thorough explanation of how rain is formed. 함정 account / as such — "is not particularly concerned with explaining rain as such"라고 명시. 비는 도입 사례일 뿐, 설명이 주목적이 아니다.
  • X ② ... the sharpness and bluntness of teeth are ultimately a matter of mere coincidence. 함정 coincidental(우연) — 아리스토텔레스는 우연 가능성을 "rejects that possibility"로 명확히 거부한다. 뉘앙스 반전.
  • O ③ Aristotle treated the regular arrangement of teeth as a sign of purpose rather than chance. 근거: "this arrangement occurs with regularity and that regularity is linked to purposefulness." 우연을 거부하고 규칙성↔목적성을 연결 — 정확한 paraphrase.
  • X ④ The blunt molars at the front of the mouth function to tear food apart. 함정 bluntness / molar* — molar는 '뒤쪽 어금니', tear는 '앞니(sharpness)'의 기능. 위치·기능을 모두 뒤집은 직역 함정.
  • X ⑤ ... natural processes unfold by accident and serve no particular end. 함정 purposefulness / for the sake of — "occur for the sake of something; that is the final cause"와 정반대. 목적론을 무목적으로 반전.
풀이 전략 일치 문제는 각주 단어(query·bluntness·molar)와 거부·부정 동사(rejects)가 함정의 핵심이다. "rejects that possibility"처럼 글이 부정하는 내용을 선지가 긍정으로 베끼면 오답(②), 각주 단어의 위치·기능을 뒤집으면 오답(④)이다.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  AI is a prolific idea generator. Human brainstorming groups tend to focus on the quality of creative ideas and almost immediately lean into variations of first suggestions, but good ideas are more likely when groups focus first on quantity. As Linus Pauling put it, "The best way to have a good idea is to have lots of ideas." Psychologist Dean Keith Simonton called this the "equal odds rule" arguing that creative success is correlated to the total number of works created. Many studies have confirmed that creative quality comes from quantity of ideas (what Bob Sutton at a design and innovation consulting company called "idea ratio"), and one Stanford study pegged the number of ideas that distinguish a successful product from a failure at more than two thousand. This is why design thinking and innovation are so focused on the processes (what Utley and Klebahn call "idea flow") of creating possibilities without judgment.

* prolific: 많이 만들어 내는, 다작의   ** peg: 못박다

  • ① AI is described as capable of generating ideas in great abundance.
  • ② Left to themselves, human groups are inclined to settle quickly into variations of their first ideas.
  • ③ The "equal odds rule" holds that a creator's success depends on the quality, not the quantity, of the works produced.
  • ④ A Stanford study put the gap between a successful and a failed product at over two thousand ideas.
  • ⑤ Design thinking values producing many possibilities before applying any judgment to them.

2 Ex 2 해설 — 내용 불일치

정답: ③ The "equal odds rule" holds that success depends on the quality, not the quantity, of works.

Simonton의 법칙은 "success is correlated to the total number of works"로 양(quantity)에 비례한다. "quality, not quantity"는 글을 정반대로 비튼 거짓.
