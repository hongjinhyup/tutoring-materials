1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  Women's dresses from the eighteenth century offered a great opportunity for repurposing, as they were frequently fashioned from long, uncut lengths of fabric. Textiles were significantly more costly than the labor it took to alter them, and as a result, most garments were modified numerous times to keep up with changes in fashion. "Even the wealthy saved fabrics, remodeled clothes, and sold unwanted items in the secondhand market," observes fashion scholar Linda Welters. "Sustainability was a way of life." Indeed, although change is inherent to fashion, the remodeling of the existing clothing, rather than buying new, was a widespread practice. It is rare, in fact, to find an eighteenth-century dress that does not show some sign of alteration. The cost of having a gown made, or remade, was only about half the cost of the textile itself and could be significantly less, depending on the type of fabric. In addition to updated dress silhouettes, accessories and trimmings played an important role in refreshing old clothing styles. For example, the addition of a row of lace on an upper part of a gown was an economical alternative to the full replacement of the gown. Many eighteenth-century dresses, particularly those from the first half of the century, were constructed with relatively crude stitches, indicating that the effortless alteration of the original gown was a consideration. Because fabrics retained value even when they were not in fashion, hand-woven silks, cherished for their quality and beauty, were sometimes passed down through generations.

* garment: 의복   ** crude: 조잡한   *** cherish: 소중히 여기다

  • ② Most eighteenth-century dresses were preserved in their original form without later changes.
  • ③ Remaking a gown typically cost only about half as much as the fabric required for it.
  • ④ Only the poor, unlike the wealthy, remade and reused their clothing.

1 Ex 1 해설 — 내용 일치

정답: ③ Remaking a gown typically cost only about half as much as the fabric required for it.

드레스를 다시 만드는 비용은 보통 그 천 값의 절반 정도에 불과했다 → 지문과 일치

정답 근거 — 핵심 어휘로 본 대조
핵심어뜻지문 ↔ 선지 대조
costly비용이 많이 드는"Textiles were ~ more costly than the labor" → 천이 노동보다 비쌌다 (선지 ①의 방향과 반대)

선지별 분석

  • X ① The labor required to alter a dress cost more than the fabric. 방향이 뒤집힌 함정. 지문은 "Textiles were significantly more costly than the labor" — 천이 노동보다 더 비쌌다. 선지는 more의 주체를 노동으로 바꿔 정반대로 만들었다.
  • X ② Most dresses were preserved in their original form without changes. "It is rare ~ to find a dress that does not show some sign of alteration" — 변형되지 않은 드레스를 찾기가 드물다. 즉 대부분 변형되었으므로 preserved / original form은 모순이다.
  • O ③ Remaking a gown typically cost only about half as much as the fabric required for it. "The cost of having a gown made, or remade, was only about half the cost of the textile itself"의 정확한 paraphrase(textile→fabric, made/remade→remaking). 비용의 크기·방향이 모두 일치한다.
  • X ④ Only the poor, unlike the wealthy, remade and reused their clothing. "Even the wealthy saved fabrics, remodeled clothes" — 부유한 사람들조차 재활용했다. 재활용을 빈곤층에 한정한 Only the poor는 'even(~조차)'의 함의를 오독한 것이다.
  • X ⑤ Hand-woven silks were usually discarded once they went out of fashion. "Because fabrics retained value even when they were not in fashion, hand-woven silks ~ were ~ passed down through generations" — 유행이 지나도 가치를 유지해 세대를 거쳐 물려졌다. discarded(폐기)는 정반대다.
함정 일치하는 선지 ③은 지문 표현을 그대로 베끼지 않고(half the cost of the textile → half as much as the fabric) 바꿔 쓴다. 반대로 오답들은 지문의 단어를 그대로 쓰되 방향만 뒤집는다(more costly, preserved, only the poor, discarded). "익숙한 단어 = 정답"이라는 직감을 경계하라.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

26005-0099 / 0100 원문 p.115 · Ex 2 難 상

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  The rise of internet-based technologies means that news is produced, distributed, and consumed faster than ever before. However, the reliability of this news is questionable. Not all organizations or individuals putting out information spend time checking the details or putting the facts into a wider context.

  People are confronted with overwhelming amounts of information not just on a daily basis, but via minute-by-minute updates transmitted to audiences around the globe. More than ever before, people are involved in organizing and managing the amount and type of news they receive. The internet means that irrespective of where we are in the world or what time of the day it is, news can be accessed from broadcasters located anywhere on the planet. As "new media" such as mobile phones, computers, and other portable devices have become more affordable, compact, and embedded in everyday life, they have come to replace television and newspapers. For younger people, advances in technology form part of the world they were born into. But the ease of use and affordability of new media means that they are an integral feature of the lives of people of all ages.

  For US sociologist Matthew Hindman, technological advances have made news more "democratic," meaning that now people of all ages and from all social backgrounds can access it. Others fear that new media poses a threat. Information on the internet is often unregulated, so while it is more accessible than ever before, its reliability is difficult to discern.

* embed: 깊숙이 자리 잡게 하다   ** discern: 분별하다

메모 공간 — 참(O)/거짓(X)을 표시하고, 거짓인 선지가 어느 문장과 모순되는지 적어 보세요
풀이 포인트 불일치 문제는 거짓 하나를 찾는 것이다. 양/범위를 나타내는 한정어(All / Not all / only / every)와 긍정·부정의 방향이 흔한 함정이다. "Not all = 전부는 아니다"를 "All = 전부"로 오독하지 않도록 주의한다.

선지별 분석

  • 참 ① New media is an integral feature for people of all ages. "the ease of use and affordability of new media means that they are an integral feature of the lives of people of all ages" — 모든 연령대에 필수적. 'all ages'를 정확히 이해하면 참.
  • 참 ② Portable devices have come to replace television and newspapers. "portable devices ~ have come to replace television and newspapers"의 직접 진술. 참.
  • 거짓 ③ All organizations ~ take time to check the details first. 지문은 "Not all organizations or individuals ~ spend time checking the details" — '전부는 아니다(일부는 확인 안 한다)'. 이를 'All(전부 확인한다)'로 뒤집은 거짓 진술이므로 정답이다. 한정어 Not all → All 오독 함정.
  • 참 ④ Hindman: advances made news more "democratic." "For US sociologist Matthew Hindman, technological advances have made news more 'democratic'"의 정확한 진술. 참.
  • 참 ⑤ More accessible, but reliability is difficult to discern. "while it is more accessible than ever before, its reliability is difficult to discern" — 접근성↑ vs 신뢰성 판단 어려움. 글의 양면성 결론과 일치하는 참.
함정 불일치 정답 ③의 함정은 단 한 단어, Not all → All이다. 양화사(quantifier)는 일치추론의 단골 오답 장치다. 지문의 Not all(부분 부정), some / often 같은 표현이 선지에서 All / every / always로 바뀌면 거짓일 확률이 높다.
