1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  In academia, you are taught to write up your results thus: "I came up with a hypothesis, then I did this experiment, and then I did another experiment, then I tested my ideas some more, and so on until [dramatic fanfare] ta-da! I proved this." If you wrote this way online, very few of your readers would make it to your dramatic conclusion, no matter how exciting or groundbreaking it was. Online, we don't have the patience to wait for your big reveal at the end. Instead, you need to tell us up front: "I proved this! And here's how I know it..." Your readership may still dwindle and trail off over the course of your writing, but at least the vast majority know what it is you've achieved from the very beginning. A few more of them may stay along for the ride if the opening line — the "hook," in journalistic terms — is particularly interesting. If you grab their attention early, you can more likely convince more readers to stay for the whole story.

* dwindle: (점점) 줄어들다

  • ① Academic writing is taught to present its conclusion before describing the experiments.
  • ② Online readers are willing to wait patiently for a dramatic conclusion if the topic is groundbreaking.
  • ③ A striking opening line guarantees that the entire readership will finish the whole story.
  • ④ Even when the main point is stated up front, some readers may still drift away before the end.
  • ⑤ The author recommends writing online in the same step-by-step way as in academia.

선지별 분석

  • X ① Academic writing is taught to present its conclusion before describing the experiments. 불일치 — 학계에서는 "hypothesis → experiment → ... → ta-da! I proved this"의 순서로, 결론(증명)을 맨 끝(at the end)에 둔다. "결론을 실험보다 먼저"는 순서를 거꾸로 읽은 함정이다.
  • X ② Online readers are willing to wait patiently for a dramatic conclusion if the topic is groundbreaking. 불일치 — 원문은 "no matter how exciting or groundbreaking it was, very few readers would make it"이고 "we don't have the patience to wait." 획기적이어도 기다리지 않는다고 했으므로 "기꺼이 인내한다"는 정반대이다.
  • X ③ A striking opening line guarantees that the entire readership will finish the whole story. 불일치 — 원문은 "A few more of them may stay"로, 흥미로운 hook은 "몇 명 더"를 머물게 할 "가능성"일 뿐이다. "전체 독자를 보장한다"는 과장 왜곡이다.
  • O ④ Even when the main point is stated up front, some readers may still drift away before the end. 일치 — "Your readership may still dwindle and trail off over the course of your writing." 결론을 처음(up front)에 제시해도 독자 이탈은 여전히 일어날 수 있다는 원문을 정확히 패러프레이즈한 유일한 선지이다.
  • X ⑤ The author recommends writing online in the same step-by-step way as in academia. 불일치 — "Instead, you need to tell us up front"에서 보듯, 글은 학술적 단계식 서술과 반대로 결론을 먼저 제시하라고 권한다. "같은 방식"은 글의 요지와 정면으로 어긋난다.
함정 일치 문제의 1순위 함정은 정도(degree) 과장이다. 원문의 "may"(가능)를 선지가 "guarantee / entire"(보장·전부)로 바꾸면 거의 항상 오답이다. ②·③이 모두 이 유형이며, 정답 ④는 원문의 "may still"을 "may still drift away"로 강도까지 그대로 옮겼다는 점에 주목한다.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

26005-0078 원문 p.95 · Ex 2 難 상

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  The human perceptual system is designed to recognize objects under changing illuminations, situations, and contexts. It is extraordinarily adapted to a constantly changing world. To deal with this uncertainty, the brain uses not one, but multiple routes. If one is blocked, it takes another route. This immense flexibility of the brain to rely on changing cues, according to whatever is available, is known as vicarious functioning. It can be found in most biological systems. In extreme cases, only a few lines are sufficient, as when we recognize the face of a celebrity in caricatures made with just a few pencil strokes. Face recognition also functions even when parts of a face are covered and internal features such as eyes are hardly visible. In this case, the brain relies on external features such as hair and head shape. For instance, migrating birds fly thousands of miles and may rely on the stars to navigate. But if it's a cloudy day, they may instead rely on landmarks or their magnetic sense. Similarly, the brain can recognize a face by the shape of the eyes, nose, and mouth.

* illumination: (불)빛, 조명   ** vicarious: 대리의

  • ① The human perceptual system can identify objects despite changes in lighting and context.
  • ② When one route is blocked, the brain switches to an alternative route.
  • ③ Vicarious functioning is found exclusively in the human brain, not in other organisms.
  • ④ Migrating birds may use landmarks or a magnetic sense when the stars are not visible.
  • ⑤ A few pencil strokes can be enough for us to recognize a celebrity's face in a caricature.

선지별 분석

  • X ① The human perceptual system can identify objects despite changes in lighting and context. 일치 — "designed to recognize objects under changing illuminations, situations, and contexts." 조명·맥락이 변해도 사물을 알아본다는 첫 문장과 정확히 부합한다.
  • X ② When one route is blocked, the brain switches to an alternative route. 일치 — "If one is blocked, it takes another route." 한 경로가 막히면 다른 경로를 택한다는 진술과 일치한다.
  • O ③ Vicarious functioning is found exclusively in the human brain, not in other organisms. 불일치(정답) — 원문은 "It can be found in most biological systems"라고 하여 대리 기능이 대부분의 생물학적 체계에서 발견된다고 명시한다. 뒤이은 철새 항해 예시도 인간 고유가 아님을 뒷받침한다. "오직 인간의 뇌에서만"은 범위를 왜곡한 모순이다.
  • X ④ Migrating birds may use landmarks or a magnetic sense when the stars are not visible. 일치 — "But if it's a cloudy day, they may instead rely on landmarks or their magnetic sense." 별이 보이지 않는 흐린 날 대체 단서를 쓴다는 진술과 부합한다.
  • X ⑤ A few pencil strokes can be enough for us to recognize a celebrity's face in a caricature. 일치 — "only a few lines are sufficient, as when we recognize the face of a celebrity in caricatures made with just a few pencil strokes." 몇 번의 연필 획만으로 얼굴 인식이 가능하다는 진술과 일치한다.
함정 불일치 문제의 핵심은 범위어(scope word) 검증이다. 원문의 "most(대부분)"를 선지가 "exclusively / only(오직)"로 좁히면 모순이 된다. ③이 바로 "most biological systems → exclusively in the human brain"으로 범위를 축소한 전형적 함정이며, 본문 뒤쪽의 철새 예시가 결정적 반증이 된다.
