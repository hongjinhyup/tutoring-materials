1 Ex 1 — 내용 일치 일치

다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?

  There are estimated to be more than 160,000 farms spread across Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland combined. On all of those farms, you will find barns, and in some of those barns, you might be fortunate enough to stumble across a classic tractor like the Massey Ferguson 35. Production of the Massey Ferguson 35 in the UK and the USA stopped in 1964. If you were to strike up a conversation with a farmer who still owns one, she might well give the chassis of her Massey Ferguson 35 an affectionate tap and nostalgically remark, 'They don't make 'em like that anymore.' The fact that the tractor has survived at least fifty-eight years seems to provide evidence of its superior build quality. But wait — how long did the other 388,381 Massey Ferguson 35 tractors produced between 1956 and 1964 last before they went for scrap? Rather than build quality, it might well be the fondness that people in Ireland have for the little red tractor (Harry Ferguson, who developed it, was born in Ireland) that has led farmers there to hold on to their Massey Fergusons and take care of them as well as they have.

* stumble across: ~을 우연히 발견하다   ** chassis: 차대(자동차의 기본 프레임 구조)   *** scrap: 폐품

  • ① Production of the Massey Ferguson 35 in the UK and the USA continued until 1956.
  • ② All of the 388,381 tractors built between 1956 and 1964 have survived to the present day.
  • ③ The author concludes that the tractor's long survival proves its superior build quality.
  • ④ Irish people's fondness for the little red tractor, rather than its build quality, likely explains why farmers there have kept and cared for it.
  • ⑤ Harry Ferguson, who developed the tractor, was born in the USA.

선지별 분석

  • X ① Production of the Massey Ferguson 35 in the UK and the USA continued until 1956. 불일치 — 원문은 "Production ~ stopped in 1964"이며, 1956년은 "produced between 1956 and 1964"에서 보듯 생산이 '시작된' 해이다. 두 연도를 뒤바꿔 읽게 만든 함정이다.
  • X ② All of the 388,381 tractors built between 1956 and 1964 have survived to the present day. 불일치 — 원문은 "how long did the other 388,381 ~ tractors ~ last before they went for scrap?"로, 나머지 38만여 대는 폐품이 되었음을 환기시킨다. "전부 살아남았다"는 정반대의 왜곡이다.
  • X ③ The author concludes that the tractor's long survival proves its superior build quality. 불일치 — "survived ~ seems to provide evidence of its superior build quality"는 필자가 제시한 '표면적' 해석일 뿐이다. 이어 "But wait ~ Rather than build quality"로 이를 반박하므로, "증명한다(proves)"는 필자의 최종 주장과 어긋난다.
  • O ④ Irish people's fondness for the little red tractor, rather than its build quality, likely explains why farmers there have kept and cared for it. 일치 — "Rather than build quality, it might well be the fondness that people in Ireland have for the little red tractor that has led farmers there to hold on to their Massey Fergusons and take care of them." 글의 결론을 정확히 패러프레이즈한 유일한 선지이다.
  • X ⑤ Harry Ferguson, who developed the tractor, was born in the USA. 불일치 — 괄호 속 정보 "Harry Ferguson, who developed it, was born in Ireland"에 따르면 출생지는 아일랜드이다. 본문에 등장한 지명(UK·USA)을 끌어다 붙인 세부 정보 함정이다.
함정 일치 문제에서 "seems to(처럼 보인다)"로 제시된 진술은 필자의 최종 주장이 아닐 가능성이 높다. 이 글은 "But wait / Rather than"이라는 전환 신호 뒤에 진짜 결론(④)이 온다. 표면 해석(③)과 연도·수치(①②), 각주성 세부 정보(⑤)를 사실과 대조해 걸러낸다.

2 Ex 2 — 내용 불일치 불일치

26005-0092 원문 p.109 · Ex 2 難 상

다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

  During crises that present immediate danger (e.g., natural disasters, acts of violence or terrorism, transportation crises), people seek trusted and verified sources to ease their immediate concerns, thus turning to traditional sources such as television and radio. For other crisis types, such as public health and political crises, however, people are more likely to turn to social media where they can seek multiple voices and a variety of expert opinions, as, unlike the first set of crises, public health and political crises may be more subjective and less straightforward, and thus necessitate a deeper dive into supplementary information and evidence. In addition to the type of crisis at hand, Liu, a communication scholar, and colleagues argued that traditional and social media have different effects on different audiences regarding the motivation to follow instructions and recommended behaviors during crises. For example, although younger audiences seem to favor social media to traditional media sources during times of crisis, older audiences indicate a preference for traditional media. Thus, both traditional and social media play a prominent role in delivering important scientific information to the public during crises.
  • ① During crises posing immediate danger, people tend to rely on traditional sources such as television and radio.
  • ② Public health and political crises tend to be more subjective and less clear-cut than immediate-danger crises.
  • ③ Older audiences, much like younger ones, prefer social media during times of crisis.
  • ④ Liu and colleagues argued that traditional and social media affect different audiences differently.
  • ⑤ The author concludes that both traditional and social media play an important role in delivering scientific information during crises.

선지별 분석

  • X ① During crises posing immediate danger, people tend to rely on traditional sources such as television and radio. 일치 — "During crises that present immediate danger ~ thus turning to traditional sources such as television and radio." 즉각적 위험 상황에서 전통 매체에 의지한다는 진술과 부합한다.
  • X ② Public health and political crises tend to be more subjective and less clear-cut than immediate-danger crises. 일치 — "public health and political crises may be more subjective and less straightforward." straightforward(명확한)를 clear-cut으로 바르게 옮긴, 핵심어의 정확한 이해를 요구하는 참 선지이다.
  • O ③ Older audiences, much like younger ones, prefer social media during times of crisis. 불일치(정답) — 원문은 "younger audiences seem to favor social media ~ older audiences indicate a preference for traditional media"로, 젊은 층과 나이 든 층의 선호가 대비된다. 나이 든 층까지 소셜 미디어를 선호한다는 것은 정반대의 모순이다.
  • X ④ Liu and colleagues argued that traditional and social media affect different audiences differently. 일치 — "Liu ~ and colleagues argued that traditional and social media have different effects on different audiences." 학자들의 주장을 그대로 패러프레이즈한 참 선지이다.
  • X ⑤ The author concludes that both traditional and social media play an important role in delivering scientific information during crises. 일치 — "Thus, both traditional and social media play a prominent role in delivering important scientific information to the public during crises." 결론 문장과 정확히 일치한다.
함정 불일치 문제의 핵심은 대비 구조(contrast)의 방향 검증이다. 원문은 "younger → social media" vs "older → traditional media"로 세대별 선호가 정반대임을 명시한다. ③은 이 대비를 "older도 younger처럼 social media"로 뒤집은 전형적 모순이며, "older audiences indicate a preference for traditional media" 한 문장이 결정적 반증이 된다.
